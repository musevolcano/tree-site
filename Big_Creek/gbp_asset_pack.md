## 11. VERIFICATION METHOD RECOMMENDATION

### Recommended: Video Verification

As of 2026, Google has largely shifted away from postcard verification for service-area businesses and encourages video verification as the primary path. For Big Creek, video is the right call.

**Why video over postcard:**
- Postcard delivery to a service-area business (no storefront) can take 14+ days and frequently fails
- Phone verification is no longer available for most new or re-verified profiles
- Video verification completes in 24–72 hours with a clear recording

**How to complete video verification:**
1. From the GBP dashboard, select "Verify now" → "Record a video"
2. Google requires a continuous, uncut video showing:
   - Your physical location (truck, yard, job site with address visible, or home address)
   - Business equipment (chainsaw, chipper, stump grinder, truck with signage)
   - Proof you operate the business (show yourself, tools, a recent invoice or work order on a tablet)
3. Upload directly in the GBP app. Review takes 24–72 hours.

**Address verification gotchas to avoid:**
- **Do not list a P.O. box as your address.** GBP will reject it.
- **If you use a home address** and want it hidden from the public, select "I deliver goods and services to my customers" during setup and hide the address — Google still verifies it but does not display it.
- **NAP consistency is critical.** The name, address, and phone number on your GBP must match exactly what is on your website and every other directory listing. Big Creek uses: `Big Creek Tree Service | (216) 551-6445 | bigcreektreeservice.com`. Do not use abbreviations, alternate phone numbers, or URL variations on any other listing.
- **Do not create a second GBP listing** for the same business. If a duplicate exists, report it for removal through the GBP dashboard before verifying the primary profile.
- **Suspend risk:** GBP profiles for service-area businesses (no storefront) are suspended more frequently than storefront businesses. If suspended, respond immediately through the GBP reinstatement form with proof of business operation (insurance certificate, business registration, recent invoice). Do not create a new profile — reinstate the existing one.
