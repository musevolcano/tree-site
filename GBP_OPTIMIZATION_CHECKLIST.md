## STEP 5 — Upload 10+ Photos (Do This First Week)
Google rewards profiles with recent, real photos. AI engines use these for trust.

**Upload these categories:**
- [ ] 3–5 job site photos (trees being removed, land clearing, stump grinding)
- [ ] 2–3 before/after photos (most powerful — shows results)
- [ ] 1–2 crew photos (builds trust — real people)
- [ ] 1 truck/equipment photo
- [ ] Your logo (once it's designed)

**Photo tips:**
- Use real job site photos — not stock photos
- Landscape orientation preferred
- Filename them before upload: `tree-removal-strongsville-ohio.jpg`, `stump-grinding-cleveland.jpg` (this helps SEO)
- Add a description to each photo when uploading

## STEP 7 — Post Weekly Updates
Go to: Posts → Add Update (takes 5 minutes)

Post once a week. Rotate through these themes:
- **Job completion:** "Just finished a large oak removal in Strongsville — before/after photos" + photo
- **Seasonal tip:** "Spring is the best time to assess storm damage from winter — call for a free inspection"
- **Service highlight:** "Did you know we offer 24/7 storm damage response? No job too big, no hour too late."
- **Review response:** Thank reviewers publicly — it signals active management

**Why this matters:** Google's AI weights recency. A profile with weekly posts ranks higher than one that's dormant.

## STEP 8 — Respond to Every Review
Go to: Reviews → Respond

For each existing 5-star review, respond like this:

> "Thank you [Name]! It was a pleasure working on your property in [city]. We're glad we could [reference what they mentioned]. Don't hesitate to call us again — we're always here. — Big Creek Tree Service (216) 551-6445"

Responding to reviews:
- Signals active management to Google's algorithm
- Shows up in AI search results as proof of responsiveness
- Encourages more reviews

## STEP 9 — Request More Reviews (Ongoing)
After every completed job, text the customer:

> "Hey [Name], thanks for choosing Big Creek Tree Service! If you're happy with the work, we'd really appreciate a quick Google review — it helps us a lot. Here's the link: [your GBP review link]"

GBP Dashboard → Get More Reviews → Copy the short link

**Target:** Get to 10+ reviews as fast as possible. At 10 reviews, Google starts showing star ratings in search results. At 25+, you start appearing in AI search citations regularly.
